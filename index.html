Question 2 Understanding (Comprehension)
Keywords: Explain

Focus: Block diagram of computer & system architecture

Explain the flow of data, instructions, and control signals within the functional block diagram of a computer, detailing the interaction between the CPU, memory unit, and I/O interface.

Interactive Computer Architecture Block Diagram

Figure 2.1: Tri-bus architecture showing bidirectional Data/Address lines and Control signal routing.

View System Signal Interaction Breakdown

1. Control Signals: Generated by the Control Unit (CU) to synchronize fetch-decode-execute cycles, control Memory Read/Write, and select I/O ports.

2. Data Lines: Bi-directional paths transferring raw operands and computed results between CPU Registers, Main Memory, and I/O buffers.

3. Instruction Flow: Instructions are fetched from RAM via address/data buses into the Instruction Register (IR), where CU decodes them into micro-operations.

Question 7 Evaluating (Evaluation)
Keywords: Critique

Focus: Critical analysis of sensory input devices under adverse conditions

Critique the operational reliability and security vulnerabilities of vision-input devices (facial recognition) compared to voice recognition devices when deployed as primary biometric authentication tools in high-ambient-noise, publicly exposed environments.

Vision-Input (Facial Recognition)

Reliability in Noise: Unaffected by high ambient sound level (>85dB).
Adverse Conditions Vulnerability: Fails under poor lighting, harsh direct sunlight glare, or face masks/glasses.
Security Spoofing Risk: 2D photo prints or high-res video playback (mitigated by 3D LiDAR/Structured Light depth sensors).

Voice Recognition Biometrics

Reliability in Noise: Severe degradation in high-ambient public environments due to signal distortion and acoustic interference.
Health Variance: Colds, sore throats, or stress significantly alter vocal resonance patterns.
Security Spoofing Risk: High risk from generative AI voice clones and parabolic microphone eavesdropping.
